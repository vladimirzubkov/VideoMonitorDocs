Usage scenarios

VideoMonitor is flexible, but you do not have to build complex commands from scratch. Here are a few common ways to use the program right out of the box.

1. Archiving raw footage​

If you have a folder full of heavy video files from a camera or phone, you can shrink them to save space.

  1. Go to the Runners tab.
  2. Put the folder with your videos in the Source field.
  3. Choose a preset like H.265 compact from the preset bank on the right.
  4. Pick an Output folder so your original files stay safe.
  5. Press Start.

The runner will go through the files one by one and save the compressed versions.

2. Downloading a playlist or a channel​

You can download an entire YouTube playlist or check a channel for new videos.

  1. Go to the Miners tab.
  2. Paste the URL of the playlist or channel into the Source field.
  3. Pick a preset like Up to 1080p or Audio only.
  4. Set an Output folder.
  5. Press Drill.

If you tick the Monitor checkbox, the miner will remember what it already downloaded. Next time you press Drill, it will only fetch the new videos.

3. Download and convert to audio (Linking)​

You can tell a runner to wait for a miner and process the files as soon as they finish downloading. This is great for downloading podcasts and converting them to a specific format.

  1. Set up a Miner to download your videos.
  2. Set up a Runner (for example, Runner A) with the Audio only (AAC) preset.
  3. On the miner row, find the linking dropdown and select Runner A.
  4. Press Drill on the miner.

When the miner finishes downloading a file, Runner A will automatically pick it up and extract the audio.

4. Heavy encoding overnight​

Encoding takes a lot of CPU power. If you need to use your computer during the day, you can tell VideoMonitor to work only at night.

  1. Press F2 to turn on shortcuts, then press T to open the schedule.
  2. Select the hours when the program is allowed to work (for example, 01:00 to 07:00).
  3. Start your runners.

They will wait quietly and show as Scheduled. When the allowed time comes, they will wake up, do the work, and pause again if the time runs out.

5. Screen recording​

You can record your desktop directly from the program without setting up OBS.

  1. Go to the Runners tab.
  2. Pick the Screen record (1080p cap) preset.
  3. Press Start.

The runner will record your screen and save it to the output folder. Press Stop when you are done.
